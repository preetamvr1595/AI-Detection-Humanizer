Fact verification systems increasingly rely on structured knowledge graphs to validate claims made within a document. By representing entities and their relationships as nodes and edges, a knowledge graph allows automated reasoning engines to check whether a stated fact is consistent with known, verified information. However, knowledge graphs are only as complete and current as the data used to build them, meaning claims about very recent events or niche topics may not be verifiable through graph reasoning alone. Combining knowledge graph lookups with live web retrieval helps close this gap, producing a hybrid fact-checking pipeline that balances structured reasoning with up-to-date information.
